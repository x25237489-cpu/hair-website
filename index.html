Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Have another question? Message us and we'll help clarify the details.

How do I order a wig?

Choose a hair category and use the enquiry link to start a WhatsApp conversation. You can ask about options, availability and pricing before placing an order.

Do you ship internationally?

Worldwide shipping enquiries are welcome. Shipping eligibility, costs, timing and delivery arrangements depend on your destination and are confirmed directly.

Can I arrange a pickup?

Pickup enquiries are welcome. The location and collection arrangements will be confirmed with you directly.

How do I book a wig installation or revamp?

Select the service and send a WhatsApp enquiry. Appointment availability, service location, pricing and any deposit requirements must be confirmed directly.

How do payments work?

Bank transfer is an accepted payment method. Confirm the final amount and verified payment details directly with Asterha Luxehair before sending any money. This website does not collect payments.

What is your returns or cancellation policy?

Please request the current returns, cancellations and appointment policy before ordering or booking. These terms have not yet been published on the website.
